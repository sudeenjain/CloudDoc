2. What the web page stores in your browser

The page uses your browser's local storage for three settings: the backend address you entered, whether the left panel is collapsed, and whether the Quick Analysis section is collapsed. These stay on your device. The page does not set cookies.

Your conversation is kept in memory only. It is gone when you reload the page or select Clear. Export saves a file to your own device.

3. What the page sends, and to whom

The page sends your PDF files, your questions, and requests to view or delete a document to one place: the backend address shown under Backend settings. It does not send data to analytics, advertising or any other third-party service, and it does not load fonts, scripts or images from other sites.

4. The backend

Who can access your documents depends on where the backend runs.

[[KEEP ONE OPTION AND DELETE THE OTHER]]

Option A, backend on your own computer. Your PDFs, questions and answers stay on your computer. We do not receive them.

Option B, backend hosted by us. We store the PDF files you upload, the text extracted from them, and a search index built from that text, on servers run by [[HOSTING PROVIDER]] in [[REGION]]. We keep them for [[RETENTION PERIOD]] or until you delete them. Only [[WHO HAS ACCESS]] can access them.

5. Deleting your documents

The delete button next to a document removes the PDF and its search index from the backend's storage. [[STATE WHETHER BACKUPS EXIST AND HOW LONG THEY ARE KEPT]]

6. The language model

Answers are written by a language model from passages found in your documents. The model name is shown in the left panel. [[CONFIRM AND STATE: the model runs on the same machine as the backend, and no document text is sent to an outside AI provider]]
